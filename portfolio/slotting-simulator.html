How the model works

01

Profile demand

Rank every SKU by pick velocity (picks/day) pulled from WMS history. A small head of fast movers drives most of the labor.

02

Score the slots

Score each location by walking distance to pack-out and pick height, producing a cost surface across the floor.

03

Assign the golden zone

Map the fastest movers to the cheapest slots, constrained by case dimensions and weight so bulky SKUs stay feasible.

04

Minimize travel

Objective is Σ(velocity × distance). The model re-slots until that total stops falling, then reports the new layout.

Why it matters

In a distribution center, picker travel is the single largest controllable cost in the pick operation. By re-slotting to demand instead of leaving SKUs where they were first received, the same team walks less and picks more — with no new equipment and no added headcount. This POC turned that idea into a repeatable, data-driven model, built as a candidate for standardization across the DHL network.
